# Fitcheck: Project Statement

## Problem Statement

A lot of people want to keep an eye on their health, but the basic numbers that help, like BMI, daily calorie needs and body fat percentage, are hidden behind formulas most of us don't remember. Online calculators exist, but they are usually scattered across different websites, full of ads, and ask for personal details that get sent off to a server.

What's missing is one simple, offline tool that puts these calculations in one place, explains the result in plain words, and works the same way every time.

## Scope of the Project

**What Fitcheck does:**
- Calculates BMI and classifies it (underweight, healthy weight, overweight, obese).
- Calculates Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation for males and females.
- Calculates Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) from BMR and five activity levels.
- Estimates body fat percentage for adult men, adult women, boys and girls.
- Runs as a menu-driven console application that keeps going until the user chooses to exit.

**What Fitcheck does not do (out of scope for now):**
- It does not give medical diagnoses or diet plans.
- It does not store user data, create accounts, or connect to the internet.
- It does not have a graphical interface.
- It does not track progress over time.

## Target Users

- Students and young adults who are just starting to look after their fitness.
- Gym-goers and beginners who want a quick calorie estimate.
- Teachers or learners who want a clear example of how health formulas turn into code.

No technical or medical background is needed to use it.

## High-Level Features

1. **BMI calculator** with a plain-English category.
2. **BMR calculator** with gender-specific formulas.
3. **TDEE calculator** with five activity levels.
4. **Body fat estimator** for adults and children.
5. **Looping main menu** so several calculations can be done in one session.
6. **Fully offline and private**, so nothing is saved or shared.
